EMERGENCY RESPONSE PROTOCOLS — ICU STANDARDS

PROTOCOL 1: CARDIAC EMERGENCY
Trigger: HR < 40 BPM or HR > 150 BPM with symptoms
Actions:
1. Call Code Blue immediately
2. Attach cardiac monitor and 12-lead ECG
3. Establish IV access
4. Prepare defibrillator / pacemaker standby
5. Notify cardiologist on call
6. Document time of onset and all interventions

PROTOCOL 2: RESPIRATORY DISTRESS
Trigger: SpO2 < 88% or RR > 30 breaths/min
Actions:
1. Elevate head of bed 30-45 degrees
2. Apply supplemental O2 — start at 2L/min via nasal cannula
3. Escalate to non-rebreather mask if no improvement in 5 min
4. Prepare for intubation if SpO2 < 85% persists
5. Order ABG (Arterial Blood Gas) immediately
6. Notify pulmonologist/intensivist

PROTOCOL 3: HYPERTENSIVE CRISIS
Trigger: Systolic BP > 180 mmHg and/or Diastolic > 120 mmHg
Actions:
1. Do NOT lower BP too rapidly — target 25% reduction in first hour
2. Administer IV antihypertensive (Labetalol or Nicardipine per order)
3. Continuous BP monitoring every 5 minutes
4. Assess for end-organ damage: neuro, renal, cardiac
5. Notify attending physician immediately
6. 12-lead ECG and troponin panel

PROTOCOL 4: HYPOGLYCEMIC EMERGENCY
Trigger: Blood glucose < 54 mg/dL or symptomatic hypoglycemia
Actions:
1. If conscious: administer 15-20g fast-acting carbohydrates orally
2. If unconscious: IV Dextrose 50% — 25-50 mL bolus
3. Recheck glucose in 15 minutes
4. If < 70 mg/dL, repeat treatment
5. Identify and address root cause (insulin overdose, missed meal)
6. Notify endocrinologist if recurrent

PROTOCOL 5: SEPSIS ALERT
Trigger: Temp > 38.3°C or < 36°C + HR > 90 + RR > 20 + suspected infection
Actions:
1. Activate Sepsis Bundle (Hour-1 Bundle)
2. Draw blood cultures x2 before antibiotics
3. Administer broad-spectrum antibiotics within 1 hour
4. IV fluid resuscitation: 30 mL/kg crystalloid
5. Monitor urine output (target > 0.5 mL/kg/hr)
6. Lactate level — if > 2 mmol/L, ICU admission

PROTOCOL 6: HYPOTHERMIA MANAGEMENT
Trigger: Core temperature < 35°C
Actions:
1. Remove wet clothing, passive rewarming with blankets
2. Warm IV fluids (42°C) for moderate/severe cases
3. Cardiac monitoring — hypothermia causes arrhythmias
4. If temp < 30°C, active internal rewarming (warmed humidified O2)
5. Do NOT declare death until patient is warm and still unresponsive
