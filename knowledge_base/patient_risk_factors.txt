CLINICAL RISK STRATIFICATION GUIDE

HIGH-RISK PATIENT PROFILES

Profile 1: Elderly Patients (Age > 65)
- Atypical presentations common (no fever with infection)
- Lower baseline BP and HR normal variants
- Polypharmacy increases adverse event risk
- Cognitive changes may mask symptom reporting
- Fall risk elevated with dizziness/hypotension
- Monitoring frequency: Every 2 hours minimum

Profile 2: Diabetic Patients
- Blood glucose target: 140-180 mg/dL (inpatient)
- Higher infection risk — monitor temperature closely
- Silent MI possible — monitor ECG changes
- Neuropathy may blunt pain responses
- Renal function monitoring: creatinine, urine output
- Hypoglycemia risk especially with insulin therapy

Profile 3: Cardiac Patients (CHF, CAD, Post-MI)
- HR and BP targets vary by condition
- Weight monitoring — 2kg gain in 24h signals fluid overload
- SpO2 target > 94% unless COPD comorbidity
- Avoid fluid overload — limit IV fluids
- Beta-blocker patients: resting HR 50-70 BPM is acceptable

Profile 4: Respiratory Patients (COPD, Asthma, Pneumonia)
- SpO2 target 88-92% for COPD (hypoxic drive risk)
- Avoid high-flow O2 in COPD — can suppress respiratory drive
- Monitor for CO2 retention signs: drowsiness, confusion
- RR trend more important than single reading

Profile 5: Post-Surgical Patients
- First 24h: monitor every 30 min
- Pain can elevate HR and BP — treat pain before escalating
- Temperature spike at 72h post-op suggests wound infection
- SpO2 < 94% with decreased breath sounds — suspect pneumothorax

RISK SCORING — EARLY WARNING SCORE (EWS)
Score 0 (each parameter):
- HR: 51-100, RR: 12-20, Temp: 36.1-37.9°C, BP Sys: 111-149, SpO2: ≥96%

Score 1:
- HR: 41-50 or 101-110, RR: 21-24, Temp: 38-38.9°C, BP Sys: 101-110, SpO2: 94-95%

Score 2:
- HR: < 40 or 111-130, RR: > 24, Temp: ≥39°C or <36°C, BP Sys: 91-100, SpO2: 92-93%

Score 3:
- HR: > 130, BP Sys: ≤90, SpO2: ≤91%

Total EWS ≥ 5: Escalate to senior clinician immediately
Total EWS ≥ 7: Consider ICU transfer
