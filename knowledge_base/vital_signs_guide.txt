CLINICAL GUIDE: VITAL SIGNS MONITORING

HEART RATE (HR)
- Normal Adult: 60-100 BPM
- Bradycardia: < 60 BPM — may indicate heart block, hypothyroidism, or athlete adaptation
- Tachycardia: > 100 BPM — may indicate fever, anemia, dehydration, anxiety, or cardiac arrhythmia
- Critical Low: < 40 BPM — immediate intervention required
- Critical High: > 150 BPM — immediate intervention required

BLOOD PRESSURE (BP)
- Normal: Systolic 90-120 mmHg / Diastolic 60-80 mmHg
- Elevated: Systolic 120-129 mmHg
- Hypertension Stage 1: Systolic 130-139 or Diastolic 80-89 mmHg
- Hypertension Stage 2: Systolic ≥ 140 or Diastolic ≥ 90 mmHg
- Hypertensive Crisis: Systolic > 180 and/or Diastolic > 120 — emergency
- Hypotension: Systolic < 90 mmHg — may indicate shock, dehydration, or sepsis

OXYGEN SATURATION (SpO2)
- Normal: 95-100%
- Mild Hypoxemia: 91-94% — supplemental oxygen may be needed
- Moderate Hypoxemia: 86-90% — oxygen therapy required
- Severe Hypoxemia: < 85% — immediate intervention, risk of organ damage
- Note: Values may be lower in patients with chronic lung disease (COPD baseline ~88-92%)

BODY TEMPERATURE
- Normal: 36.1-37.2°C (97.0-99.0°F)
- Low-grade Fever: 37.3-38.0°C
- Fever: 38.1-39.0°C — investigate infection source
- High Fever: 39.1-40.0°C — antipyretic treatment indicated
- Hyperpyrexia: > 40.0°C — medical emergency
- Hypothermia: < 35.0°C — medical emergency, risk of cardiac arrest

RESPIRATORY RATE (RR)
- Normal Adult: 12-20 breaths/min
- Tachypnea: > 20 breaths/min — may indicate pneumonia, anxiety, metabolic acidosis
- Bradypnea: < 12 breaths/min — may indicate drug overdose or neurological issue
- Critical: > 30 breaths/min — respiratory failure risk

BLOOD GLUCOSE
- Normal Fasting: 70-100 mg/dL
- Pre-diabetes: 100-125 mg/dL
- Diabetes: ≥ 126 mg/dL (fasting)
- Hypoglycemia: < 70 mg/dL — administer glucose immediately
- Severe Hypoglycemia: < 54 mg/dL — emergency
- Hyperglycemia: > 180 mg/dL (post-meal) — insulin may be required
- Diabetic Crisis: > 300 mg/dL — immediate medical attention
